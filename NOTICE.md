## Classical sources

Astrological rules are freshly paraphrased from public-domain classical texts
(Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Brihat Jataka, Phaladeepika, Saravali, Jaimini
Upadesa Sutras, Tajika Nilakanthi, Brihat Samhita) with citations in the code
and corpus. No copyrighted modern translation text is reproduced.

## AI disclosure

Readings, palm analyses, and Vastu narratives are written by an AI model from
DETERMINISTICALLY COMPUTED chart facts and rule findings only — the model never
computes astrological values. All output is offered for guidance and
reflection, not as a substitute for professional advice.
